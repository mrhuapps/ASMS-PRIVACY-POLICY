Privacy Policy

ASMS — Advance School Management System

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ASMS, developed by Mr. HU Apps, handles information when you use the application.

2. Information You Enter

Depending on usage, ASMS may store information entered by an authorised school administrator, including:

  • Principal and administrator names, usernames and phone numbers.
  • School name, address, phone number and email.
  • Student names, class details and guardian contact information.
  • Teacher names, contact information and subjects.
  • Attendance, fees, classes, sections and subjects.
  • Student photos, teacher photos and school logos added by users.

Schools are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate permission to enter and manage student information, including children's information.

3. Data Storage

In the current version, application records are stored locally on the device using IndexedDB and Local Storage. The application also includes local fallback storage.

Android device backup is permitted by the app configuration. Depending on device settings, some app data may be included in Android backups.

We do not claim that local data is end-to-end encrypted. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or its app data may potentially access stored information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide school-management functions, including student and teacher records, attendance, fee management, reports and receipts. Contact numbers may be used to prepare SMS messages when the user chooses that feature.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

ASMS does not include a feature intended to sell personal information. When the SMS feature is used, the app opens the device's SMS composer. The user reviews and sends the message through their mobile carrier.

The app requests Internet and network-state permissions. These permissions alone do not mean school records are uploaded. The current data layer uses local device storage.

If future versions introduce cloud synchronisation, analytics, advertisements or other third-party services, this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Photos and Permissions

Users may select photos and logos through the Android file or photo picker. These are stored as part of the app's local data. The current Android manifest does not declare camera, contacts or location permissions.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains in app storage until deleted through available app features, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Android backups may retain copies. Users should export or back up important records before clearing data or uninstalling.

8. Children's Privacy

ASMS is intended for authorised adult school administrators, not for independent use by children. Schools are responsible for handling student information appropriately and obtaining any required parent or guardian permissions.

9. Security

We take reasonable care in designing the application, but no device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their devices and administrator credentials.

10. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this webpage.
